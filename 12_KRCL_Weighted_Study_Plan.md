## 3. 6-week countdown plan (Aug → Sept CBT)

### Week 1 — Foundation (Technical base)
- Building Materials + Concrete + Construction  
- Surveying basics (chain, compass, levelling)  
- Maths: %, ratio, LCM/HCF, BODMAS, averages  

### Week 2 — Structures + Maths speed
- SOM (stress, strain, SFD/BMD)  
- RCC + Steel basics  
- Maths: Time-Work, Speed-Distance, SI-CI, Profit-Loss  

### Week 3 — Fluids + Soil
- Fluid properties, Bernoulli, pipes  
- Soil index properties, compaction, foundations  
- Reasoning: series, coding, directions, blood relation  

### Week 4 — Transport + Environment + Estimation
- Highway SSD/OSD, flexible/rigid, railway BG  
- Water supply, sewage, BOD, estimate/tender  
- GA: Polity, Geography, Freedom struggle, Current affairs 20 min/day  

### Week 5 — Full syllabus revision + mocks
- 3 sectional tests (Technical 50Q / Non-tech 50Q)  
- 2 full mocks (90 min)  
- Weak-topic surgery only  

### Week 6 — Final polish
- Formula sheet daily  
- 2–3 full mocks  
- Light revision only (no new heavy topics)  
- Sleep well before exam week

| Minutes | Action |
|---------|--------|
| 0–2 | Calm breath, check language (Kannada/English), skim sections |
| 2–40 | **Technical first** (50Q) — attempt sure ones |
| 40–65 | Maths (20Q) — high accuracy |
| 65–80 | Reasoning + GA (20Q) |
| 80–88 | Science (10Q) |
| 88–90 | Review marked doubts; skip high-risk guesses |

**Guessing rule (negative −1):**  
- If you can eliminate to **2 options** → calculated guess OK  
- If totally blank → **leave it** (safer than −1)
