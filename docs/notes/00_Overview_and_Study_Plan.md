# Junior Engineer (Civil) — Exam Prep Guide
# ಜೂನಿಯರ್ ಎಂಜಿನಿಯರ್ (ಸಿವಿಲ್) — ಪರೀಕ್ಷಾ ತಯಾರಿ ಮಾರ್ಗದರ್ಶಿ

**Language:** English + ಕನ್ನಡ | **Level:** Easy to understand | **Focus:** Objective / MCQ

---

## 1. What this exam covers | ಈ ಪರೀಕ್ಷೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಏನು ಬರುತ್ತದೆ

Questions are **objective type with multiple choices** for:
- Junior Engineer / Civil
- Junior Engineer / Mechanical

| Part | Subject | English | ಕನ್ನಡ |
|------|---------|---------|-------|
| A | General Science | Basic Class 10 science | 10ನೇ ತರಗತಿ ವಿಜ್ಞಾನ ಮೂಲಭೂತ |
| B | Basic Mathematics | Arithmetic, Algebra, Geometry, etc. | ಅಂಕಗಣಿತ, ಬೀಜಗಣಿತ, ಜ್ಯಾಮಿತಿ |
| C | GA + Reasoning | Current affairs, India, logic | ಸಾಮಾನ್ಯ ಜ್ಞಾನ + ತರ್ಕ |
| D | Technical (Civil) | Core civil engineering | ಸಿವಿಲ್ ಎಂಜಿನಿಯರಿಂಗ್ ವಿಷಯಗಳು |

## 2. Suggested weightage for study | ಅಧ್ಯಯನಕ್ಕೆ ಸಲಹೆ ತೂಕ

| Priority | Topic | Time share |
|----------|-------|------------|
| ★★★ | Technical Ability (Civil) | 45–50% |
| ★★★ | Basic Mathematics | 20% |
| ★★ | Reasoning | 15% |
| ★★ | General Awareness | 10% |
| ★ | General Science | 5–10% |

**Tip:** Technical + Maths decide selection. GA needs daily newspaper/current affairs.

**ಸಲಹೆ:** ತಾಂತ್ರಿಕ + ಗಣಿತ = ಆಯ್ಕೆಗೆ ಮುಖ್ಯ. ಸಾಮಾನ್ಯ ಜ್ಞಾನಕ್ಕೆ ಪ್ರತಿದಿನ ವೃತ್ತಪತ್ರಿಕೆ.

## 3. 8-week study plan | 8 ವಾರಗಳ ಅಧ್ಯಯನ ಯೋಜನೆ

### Week 1–2 | ವಾರ 1–2
- Building Materials & Construction
- Surveying & Levelling
- Maths: Number system, %, Ratio, LCM/HCF

### Week 3–4 | ವಾರ 3–4
- Strength of Materials + RCC + Steel
- Fluid Mechanics basics
- Maths: Time-Work, Speed-Distance, SI-CI, Profit-Loss

### Week 5–6 | ವಾರ 5–6
- Soil + Foundations + Highway/Rail
- Environmental + Estimation
- Reasoning practice daily (30 min)

### Week 7 | ವಾರ 7
- Full syllabus revision
- GA: Polity, Geography, Freedom struggle, Economy
- Science quick revision (Class 10)

### Week 8 | ವಾರ 8
- Mock tests (full length)
- Weak topic revision
- Formula sheet + short notes only

---

## 4. How to use these notes | ಈ ನೋಟ್‌ಗಳನ್ನು ಹೇಗೆ ಬಳಸುವುದು

1. Read **English** for concept clarity.
2. Read **ಕನ್ನಡ** to remember in mother tongue.
3. After each topic, solve **10–20 MCQs**.
4. Maintain a **formula diary** (ಗಣಿತ + ತಾಂತ್ರಿಕ ಸೂತ್ರಗಳು).
5. Revise short notes every Sunday.

## 6. Exam mindset | ಪರೀಕ್ಷಾ ಮನೋಭಾವ

- Read question fully → then options.
- Eliminate wrong choices first.
- Do not spend too long on one question.
- Attempt known topics first (Technical + Maths).

**ನೆನಪಿಡಿ:** ಸರಳ ಸೂತ್ರ + ನಿಯಮಿತ ಅಭ್ಯಾಸ = ಯಶಸ್ಸು.
