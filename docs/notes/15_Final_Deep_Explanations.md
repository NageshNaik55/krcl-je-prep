# 1. General Science | ಸಾಮಾನ್ಯ ವಿಜ್ಞಾನ

## What exam expects | ಪರೀಕ್ಷೆ ಏನು ಕೇಳುತ್ತದೆ
Class 10 CBSE basics: motion, force, energy, light, electricity, acids-bases, metals, life processes, environment. Not engineering physics — **definitions + units + simple relations**.

## Deep explanation (Physics)

### Force, work, energy — one story
Imagine pushing a box:
- **Force** makes velocity change → F = ma  
  ಬಲ = ದ್ರವ್ಯರಾಶಿ × ವೇಗವರ್ಧನೆ
- If the box **moves** in force direction → **Work** W = F × s  
  ಚಲನೆಯ ದಿಕ್ಕಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಬಲ → ಕೆಲಸ
- If force is **perpendicular** to motion (like circular string tension sideways to instantaneous displacement idea in basics) → work can be zero in school definition when displacement ⊥ force.
- Energy is “ability to do work”: moving → KE = ½mv²; height → PE = mgh.

**Teacher tip:** Same joule unit for work and energy because energy is stored work capacity.

### Electricity chain
Current I is flow of charge. Battery gives voltage V. Resistance R opposes.
- Ohm: V = IR  
- Series: resistances add (one path)  
- Parallel: reciprocals add (multiple paths → less total R)

**Real feel:** Festival lights in series — one bulb fails, all die. House wiring parallel — one bulb off, others work.

### Light & sound
- Light: reflection i = r; convex converges, concave diverges.  
- Sound needs medium; vacuum silent. Speed in air ~ 340 m/s.

## Deep explanation (Chemistry)
- pH < 7 acid, = 7 neutral, > 7 base. Litmus: acid turns blue→red.  
- Rusting = Fe + O₂ + moisture. Prevent by paint/oil/galvanizing.  
- Brass = Cu+Zn; Bronze = Cu+Sn.

## Deep explanation (Biology/Env)
- Photosynthesis in chloroplast: CO₂ + H₂O → glucose + O₂ (sunlight).  
- Heart 4 chambers; kidneys purify; ozone blocks UV; CO₂ is greenhouse gas.

## Must lock | ಅನಿವಾರ್ಯ
F=ma, W=Fs, KE=½mv², PE=mgh, V=IR, pH7 water, photosynthesis products, rust needs air+moisture.

## Exam traps
- Confusing power (Watt) with energy (Joule).  
- Saying sound travels in vacuum.  
- Mixing brass and bronze.

# 2. Basic Mathematics | ಮೂಲಭೂತ ಗಣಿತ

## What exam expects
Speed calculation under pressure. 20 Q × 3 marks = 60 marks. Accuracy > attempting all with guessing (−1).

## Deep explanation — how to think

### Percentages are fractions in disguise
10% = 1/10, 25% = 1/4, 12.5% = 1/8.  
Increase x% → multiply by (1 + x/100).  
Successive a% then b% ≠ (a+b)% exactly; extra ab/100 term.

### Time & Work is “rate”
If A finishes in a days, rate = 1/a per day.  
Together: add rates.  
Pipes: inlet +, outlet −.

### Time & Distance
D = S × T. Always match units.  
km/h → m/s × 5/18; reverse × 18/5.  
Equal distances, speeds x,y → avg = 2xy/(x+y).

### SI vs CI
SI grows **linear** with time.  
CI grows on previous amount — compound.  
For 2 years, CI − SI = P(R/100)² (standard result).

### Geometry / Trig essentials
Triangle angles 180°. Pythagoras a²+b²=c².  
sin=opp/hyp, cos=adj/hyp, tan=opp/adj.  
sin²+cos²=1. Memorize 0°,30°,45°,60°,90° table.

## Worked mini examples
1. 15% of 200 = 30.  
2. 36 km/h = 36×5/18 = 10 m/s.  
3. A=10d, B=15d → together 1/10+1/15=1/6 → 6 days.  
4. SS not needed here — keep rates clear.

## Must lock
BODMAS, HCF×LCM=product (2 numbers), SI=PRT/100, 5/18 and 18/5, WL/4 and wL²/8 appear in SOM too, tan45=1.

## Exam traps
Doing left-to-right ignoring BODMAS.  
Using SI formula for CI.  
Forgetting unit conversion in speed.

# 5. Building Materials & Construction | ಕಟ್ಟಡ ಸಾಮಗ್ರಿ

## Deep explanation — materials as a system
Building stands because materials carry load and resist weather.

### Bricks
Good brick: uniform, ringing sound, low water absorption, strength ≥ 10.5 N/mm² (1st class).  
Underburnt = soft/weak; overburnt (jhama) = distorted, sometimes used in foundations as hardcore idea.

### Cement chemistry (exam gold)
- **C3S:** early strength (first days).  
- **C2S:** later strength.  
- **C3A:** high heat of hydration.  
Initial set ≥ 30 min (working time); final ≤ 10 hr.

### Water-cement ratio (Abrams)
Less water (enough for hydration/workability) → denser paste → **higher strength**.  
Too little water → poor compaction; too much → pores → weak.

### Workability
Slump test measures ease of placing. Heavily reinforced needs higher slump.

### Concrete age
Standard cube 150 mm; 28-day strength is characteristic reference.

### DPC
Moisture rises by capillary action into walls → damp. DPC at plinth stops rising damp.

## Must lock
10.5 N/mm², initial set ≥30 min, lower w/c → higher strength, slump=workability, DPC at plinth, M20≈1:1.5:3, M15≈1:2:4.

## Exam traps
Higher w/c means higher strength (false).  
Final setting 30 min (false — that’s initial minimum).

# 6. Surveying & Levelling | ಸಮೀಕ್ಷೆ

## Deep explanation — from whole to part
You first establish accurate control (big framework), then detail. Prevents error accumulation.

### Compass bearings
WCB 0–360°. Back bearing = Fore ± 180°.  
If FB−BB ≠ 180° → local attraction (iron/current disturbing needle).

### Levelling arithmetic
HI = RL + BS  
Next RL = HI − FS (or IS)  
This is the entire book-keeping of height transfer.

### Contours
Equal elevation lines. Close spacing = steep. Closed with higher inside = hill.

### Modern tools
Theodolite: angles. Tachometry: distance from staff intercept (D≈100s+C). Total Station: angles+distance+coords. GPS: satellites.

## Must lock
Whole→part, BB=FB±180°, HI=RL+BS, close contours=steep, K≈100.

## Exam traps
HI = RL − BS.  
Thinking widely spaced contours are steep.

# 7. Mechanics & Structural | ರಚನೆ

## Deep explanation — stress to structure

### Stress & strain
Stress = load intensity (P/A). Strain = stretch ratio (δL/L).  
Within elastic limit, Hooke: stress ∝ strain → E = σ/ε.

### SFD & BMD (why engineers draw them)
Beam has shear and bending. Failure often starts where BM or shear is critical.  
Relations: dM/dx = SF (sign convention dependent). Max BM often where SF=0.

**Lock formulas:**
- SS, central point load: Mmax = WL/4  
- SS, UDL w: Mmax = wL²/8  

### RCC philosophy
Concrete strong in compression, weak in tension → steel in tension zone.  
**Under-reinforced:** steel yields first → ductile warning → preferred.  
**Over-reinforced:** concrete crushes suddenly → brittle → avoid.  
Stirrups fight shear near supports.

### Slabs
Ly/Lx > 2 → one-way (main steel short span). ≤2 → two-way.

### Steel
Compression members buckle; capacity falls with high slenderness Le/r.  
Fillet weld throat ≈ 0.7 × size.

## Must lock
σ=P/A, WL/4, wL²/8, under-reinforced preferred, Ly/Lx>2 one-way, throat≈0.7t, Fe415 fy≈415 MPa.

## Exam traps
Preferring over-reinforced.  
Mixing SF max location with BM max blindly without conditions.

# 8. Fluid Mechanics & Hydraulics | ದ್ರವಗಳು

## Deep explanation — pressure and energy

### Pressure in liquid
P = ρgh. Deeper → more pressure (dam bottom thicker conceptually).

### Continuity
Mass conserved. For incompressible: A1V1=A2V2. Narrow pipe → faster flow.

### Bernoulli (energy per unit weight)
P/γ + V²/2g + z = constant along streamline (ideal).  
Venturi, orifice, pitot use this idea.

### Pipe losses
Friction dominates long pipes: hf = fLV²/(2gD).  
Reynolds number: low → laminar (~<2000), high → turbulent (~>4000).

### Hydrology words
Rain gauge measures rainfall. Run-off = rain that flows on surface. Duty/delta relate irrigation supply to crop water.

## Must lock
P=ρgh, A1V1=A2V2, Bernoulli three heads, Re limits, rain gauge.

## Exam traps
Bernoulli without knowing each term meaning.  
Thinking continuity increases both A and V together.

# 9. Geotech & Transportation | ಮಣ್ಣು / ಸಾರಿಗೆ

## Deep explanation — soil decides foundation
Structure load eventually sits on soil. Soft clay → settlement/consolidation with time. Sand drains faster.

### Index properties
Water content w, void ratio e, porosity n, saturation S.  
Important: **Se = wG**.

### Atterberg
LL: starts flowing. PL: thread crumbles. PI = LL−PL (clayey if high).

### Compaction
Beat out air voids. OMC → MDD peak on curve. More energy → higher MDD, lower OMC typically.

### Foundations
Shallow (footing/raft) if good soil near surface. Deep (piles) if weak upper layers. Differential settlement damages buildings more than uniform.

### Highways
Camber drains water. SSD for stopping. Flexible = bituminous layers; rigid = CC slab.

### Railway
BG = 1.676 m. Sleepers hold gauge; ballast drains + cushions.

## Must lock
Se=wG, PI=LL−PL, OMC/MDD, SSD idea, BG=1.676 m, flexible vs rigid.

## Exam traps
MG and BG mix-up.  
Thinking rigid pavement is bituminous.

# 10. Environmental Engineering & Estimation | ಪರಿಸರ / ಅಂದಾಜು

## Deep explanation — water to waste to cost

### Water treatment story (sequence matters)
Raw water → screening → sedimentation → coagulation/filtration ideas → **filtration** → **chlorination** → distribution.  
Residual chlorine protects in pipes.

### Sewage
BOD = oxygen microbes need for organics (pollution indicator).  
Primary = physical settling/screening. Secondary = biological (ASP, trickling filter).

### Estimation
Detailed estimate = quantity × rate from drawings.  
Rate analysis = material + labour + equipment + overheads + profit.  
Tender pack: NIT + specs + BOQ + conditions. EMD shows seriousness.

## Must lock
Sedimentation→Filtration→Chlorination, high BOD=more pollution, primary vs secondary, rate analysis components, BOQ/NIT.

## Exam traps
Wrong treatment order.  
Thinking BOD measures hardness.
